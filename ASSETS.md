# Grove & Stone — visual sources

The supplied `foodwagon-v1.0.0.zip` was the layout and colour reference: warm yellow hero, orange CTAs, product grids, category browsing, process strip and dark footer. The app was implemented in React Native; the sample's restaurant data, meal images and brand name were not imported.

Five original assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool and copied into `mobile/assets/catalog/`. They are illustrative local-review artwork, not photographs of actual inventory. Original image files were preserved without editing. Runtime animations are implemented in React Native.

## Hero prompt

File: `mobile/assets/catalog/hero.png`

Create a premium ecommerce hero photograph asset, ads-marketing use case, no text, no logo. A generous ivory ceramic shallow bowl overflowing with ripe mango halves, pink dragon fruit cut open, kiwi halves, passionfruit, strawberries and green grapes, a few almonds and fresh green leaves. Dynamic suspended falling fruit pieces above and around the bowl, appetizing realistic texture, studio sunlight and soft shadows, elevated commercial fruit photography. Entire composition fully visible with breathing room. Solid uniform golden yellow background #FFBE21 designed to integrate into a Foodwagon-style golden yellow website hero. Square 1024x1024 composition. Fruit should occupy most of image, bowl anchored lower center. Avoid lettering, badges, UI, people, utensils.
